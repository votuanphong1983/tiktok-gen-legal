TiktokGen

TiktokGen is a personal video-publishing tool operated by Vo Tuan Phong. It produces short educational videos for the owner's own channels ("Cú Vì Sao" and "Kiến Thức Lớp 10") and uploads them to YouTube, Facebook and TikTok.

What the app does

  • Generates short educational videos (script, images, narration, rendering) on the owner's computer.
  • Uses Google OAuth with the YouTube Data API (scopes youtube.upload and youtube) so the channel owner can upload those videos to their own YouTube channel and add them to their own playlists.
  • Videos are only uploaded when the owner explicitly clicks "Publish" in a local dashboard.

Who can use it

TiktokGen is not a public service. It is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own channels. No sign-up is offered and no third-party user data is collected.
